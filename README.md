# 📊 Interactive Sales Performance Dashboard (Microsoft Excel)

An executive-ready, interactive **Sales Performance Dashboard** built in Microsoft Excel to analyze revenue, order volume, product performance, regional trends, and sales channels across 2025–2026.

https://github.com/anushkachakravarti/Excel_Sales_performance_Dashboard/blob/main/Screenshot%202026-11-16%20222015.png

---

## 🎯 Project Objective
The goal of this project is to transform raw transactional sales data into an intuitive, self-service reporting tool that enables stakeholders to monitor KPIs and filter performance dynamically across time periods, regions, product categories, customer segments, and sales channels.

---

## 📈 Key Insights & Findings
* **Headline Metrics:** **$1.27M** Total Revenue | **1,500** Total Orders | **6,504** Units Sold | **$844.58** Average Order Value.
* **Category Dominance:** **Electronics ($733K)** accounts for over half of total revenue, followed by **Furniture ($358K)** and **Clothing ($175K)**.
* **Hero Products:** The **Laptop Pro 15 ($368K)** is the top revenue generator, more than doubling the second-best product, the **4K Monitor 27" ($160K)**.
* **Channel Mix:** **Online Store ($690K)** leads sales volume (~54% of total revenue), ahead of **Wholesale ($387K)** and **Retail Shop ($190K)**.
* **Regional Balance:** **East ($344K)**, **North ($335K)**, and **South ($335K)** perform consistently, while **West ($253K)** presents growth potential.

---

## 🛠️ Features & Technical Highlights
* **Multi-Tab Navigation:** Seamless navigation buttons switching between **Dashboard**, **Summary**, and **Data** views.
* **Dynamic Slicers:** Interactive filtering by **Region**, **Category**, **Segment**, and **Sales Channel**.
* **Granular Date Timelines:** Synchronized `Order Date` timelines allowing users to drill down by **Years, Quarters, Months, or Days**.
* **Custom KPI Cards:** Linked directly to dynamic PivotTable outputs for real-time updates.

---

## 🚀 How to Use
1. Download the `.xlsx` workbook from this repository.
2. Open the file in **Microsoft Excel**.
3. Use the left-hand **Slicers** or top **Date Timelines** to slice and explore the metrics dynamically.
